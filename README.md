FastVideo DS 

A simple Android video converter made for Nintendo DS and DSi users.

FastVideo DS is designed to make converting videos for Nintendo DS easier without needing a computer. You can select videos from your Android device, adjust conversion settings, and prepare them for playback on your DS.

Features Convert videos to Nintendo DS-compatible DPG format. Optimized 256×192 resolution. Batch video conversion. Background conversion support. Adjustable video quality and bitrate. Audio configuration. Video trimming. Custom output directory. Conversion progress tracking. Conversion history. Storage management. Automatic output validation. Error detection and recovery. Support for videos up to 30 minutes. 30+ conversion and customization features. Performance 

FastVideo DS aims to make the conversion process convenient and reliable. It includes several options to control output quality, file size, and compatibility.

A small note about speed: Despite its name, FastVideo DS is not necessarily fast. Video conversion can take some time, especially with longer videos or higher-quality settings. Actual performance depends on your Android device, video resolution, encoding settings, and file size.

The goal is to provide a practical conversion experience rather than promise unrealistic conversion speeds.
